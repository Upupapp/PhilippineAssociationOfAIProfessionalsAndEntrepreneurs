Partner benefits are not guaranteed

They may change or end without notice. Every offer is subject to a confirmed agreement, member eligibility, geographic or account restrictions, redemption limits and the provider's own terms. Examples shown here must not be read as implying a partnership until it is formally confirmed.

How a benefit becomes real

  1. PAAIPE and a provider agree scope, eligibility and terms in writing.

  2. The offer is announced only after that agreement is confirmed.

  3. Eligible members access it through the Members Portal, subject to the provider's own terms.

Until those steps are complete for a given offer, nothing about it appears here — no provider name, no logo, and no amount.
