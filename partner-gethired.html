About GetHired

GetHired Online makes recruitment more effective and efficient through video interview responses. Employers can pre-screen applicants before a first meeting, cutting the back-and-forth of calling, scheduling and getting ghosted on first interviews.

Built for all kinds of jobs, the platform includes a badge system, flexible work-arrangement and compensation options, a customizable employer account and a real-time activity dashboard. Job seekers upload a CV, attach a cover letter and record video answers.

What they do

Video interview responses

Applicants answer pre-screening questions on video, so employers assess communication and fit early.

Employer dashboard

Real-time activity, customizable accounts and integrations with other systems.

Flexible job types

Badges plus multiple compensation and work-arrangement options for remote, hybrid and on-site roles.
