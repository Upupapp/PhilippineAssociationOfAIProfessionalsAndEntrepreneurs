How a partnership is agreed

  1. Share the idea.

  2. PAAIPE reviews fit, value, feasibility, and responsible-use considerations.

  3. Both parties agree on scope, eligibility, branding, data use, recognition, and terms.

  4. Announce only after formal approval.

  5. Review outcomes where appropriate.

Nothing is announced before it is approved

No partnership, endorsement or member benefit should be announced or visually represented until it has been formally approved. Product access, credits, tokens, trials and discounts remain subject to the provider's eligibility rules, limits, availability and terms.
