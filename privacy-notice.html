Who is responsible for your information

The Philippine Association of AI Professionals and Entrepreneurs (“PAAIPE”, “we”) is the personal information controller for the information described here. We decide what is collected and why.

  • Registered name and address: to confirm: PAAIPE’s registered name and office address
  • Data Protection Officer: to confirm: the DPO’s name and email address
  • General contact: to confirm: the contact email address for privacy requests

Until those are confirmed, please reach us through the contact details published on paaipe.org.

What we collect

We only collect what we need. We do not ask for sensitive personal information as defined by the Data Privacy Act — we do not collect your government identifiers, health, religion, or political affiliation — and you should not send them to us.

WhenWhat is collected
Registering for an eventYour name, email address, and optionally your role and organisation; which description best fits you; what you would like to learn and any question for the speaker; how you heard about the event; whether you agreed to event communications and whether you opted in to updates; and the date and time of your registration.
Creating an Agent accountYour name, email address and a password. We never see or store your password — it is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores it in a hashed form that we cannot read.
AutomaticallyYour browser’s user-agent string is stored with an event registration to help us spot automated abuse. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not profile you.

Why we process it, and on what basis

Under the Data Privacy Act we must have a lawful basis. Ours are:

PurposeBasis
Running an event you registered for — sending your confirmation, the Zoom access details and remindersNecessary to provide what you asked for, and your express consent given at registration
Creating and operating your Agent account and the member portalNecessary to provide the membership service you asked for
Shaping session content from what members say they want to learnOur legitimate interest in running useful sessions, balanced against your privacy
Sending news about programmes, benefits and partnersYour separate, optional consent. You can withdraw it at any time
Keeping the site secure and preventing abuseOur legitimate interest in protecting members and the service

You are never required to accept marketing to take part. Agreeing to event communications and opting in to updates are two separate choices, and the second is never ticked for you.

Scope and method of processing

Processing is electronic. The website is static and is served by Netlify. Registrations and Agent profiles are stored in Google Firestore, and accounts are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. There is no automated decision-making that produces legal effects for you, and no profiling.

Who your information is shared with

  • Google (Firebase) stores the data on our behalf as our processor. It does not use it for its own purposes.
  • Netlify serves the website. It receives ordinary web-server information such as your IP address when you load a page.
  • Zoom is used to run sessions. If you join a session, Zoom processes your information under its own terms.
  • Our partners do not receive your information unless you have specifically asked us to introduce you. Registering for an event does not share your details with any partner.

We do not sell your personal information, and we never will.

Where your information is stored, and transfers abroad

PAAIPE’s database is located in Singapore (Google Cloud region asia-southeast1), chosen as the closest region to the Philippines. Because the data leaves the country, this is a cross-border transfer, and we tell you so plainly. PAAIPE remains accountable for your information wherever it is held, and Google processes it under contractual protections. Some account and email operations may be handled in other Google regions.

How long we keep it

The Data Privacy Act requires a determined period, not an open-ended one:

  • Event registrations: kept for 24 months after the event, then deleted. to confirm: the association to confirm 24 months
  • Agent accounts: kept while your account is active, and for 12 months after you close it, then deleted. to confirm: the association to confirm 12 months
  • Update subscriptions: kept until you withdraw consent, then removed promptly.

We may keep a minimal record longer where a law requires it, and we will tell you if that applies.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • Be informed — which is what this notice is for.
  • Access your information, its sources, who received it, and how it was processed.
  • Correct anything inaccurate or out of date; we will act on this immediately.
  • Object to processing, including withdrawing consent for updates at any time.
  • Erasure or blocking — ask us to remove or block your information where the law allows.
  • Data portability — receive a copy of your information in a structured, commonly used electronic format.
  • Damages — be indemnified for damage caused by inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or unauthorised use of your information.
  • Lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

To exercise any of these, write to our Data Protection Officer using the contact details above. We will respond as promptly as we can and will not charge you for a reasonable request.

Complaining to the regulator

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your information, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission, 5th Floor, Delegation Building, PICC Complex, Roxas Boulevard, Pasay City 1307 — privacy.gov.ph. You do not need our permission to do so, and we would rather you told us first so we can put it right.

How we protect it

Access to the database is controlled by security rules that allow a registration to be created but do not allow anyone to read other people’s registrations from a browser. Your Agent profile can be read only by you. Passwords are never stored by us. Traffic is encrypted in transit. No system is perfect; if a breach ever affects your rights and freedoms we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle your information we will update this page, change the version number and effective date at the top, and say what changed. Material changes will be announced to members.
