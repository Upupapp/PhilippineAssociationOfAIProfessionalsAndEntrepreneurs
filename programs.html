Programs are announced when they’re confirmed

Dates, partners and formats for programs in development may change as they are piloted. Anything involving a partner (trainers, venues, tools) appears here only after the agreement is formally approved.

How a program comes to life

Propose

An idea from members, Agents or partners is written up with a goal, a format and who it’s for.

Pilot with members

A small first run with real members, so the format is tested before it’s promised to everyone.

Launch and repeat

What works becomes a regular program, with dates on the Events page and materials under Resources.

Want to host a Build Night, lead a Circle, or mentor?

Programs run on members. Tell PAAIPE what you’d like to help with, or propose something new.
